## Toolbox

Scope: homepage plus all 31 discovered internal pages (20 tool/card destinations, nine categories, About and the category directory). Public HTTP and server-rendered content checks only across the full set. Fresh headless Edge rendered the bill splitter, EMI calculator and password generator without captured page errors. This is not a complete functional or security audit of every tool, upload, download or account feature.

1. **Correct the EMI explanatory copy.** `/category/finance/emi-calculator` says that ₹50 lakh at 8.5% has ₹32 lakh interest over 20 years and ₹54 lakh over 30 years. Standard monthly reducing-balance calculation, with constant rate and no charges, gives about **₹54,13,878.80** and **₹88,40,442.70** respectively. The live 20-year calculator itself displays ₹43,391.16 monthly and ₹54,13,878.80 interest correctly; the observed problem is contradictory editorial copy, not that demonstrated calculation.
2. **Align prepayment examples with the actual supported input.** The introduction describes a one-time prepayment, while explanatory text discusses yearly prepayments and savings without specifying the exact simulation. Show one fully specified example using the implemented timing and recurrence, and avoid universal savings or penalty statements.
3. **Correct password-strength comparisons.** `/category/productivity/password-generator` says adding four characters makes a password roughly 10,000 times harder to guess without specifying a character distribution. For independent uniformly selected characters from a 94-character pool, four more positions multiply the space by **94^4 = 78,074,896**. Entropy and passphrase examples must state the pool/word-list size, uniformity and actual generation rules. A numeric entropy estimate is not a security certification.
4. **Investigate an intermittent route failure.** `/category/finance/tip-split` returned HTTP 500 with a Tool not found title on the first crawl. Two immediate rechecks, a full later crawl and an isolated browser visit returned 200. Preserve the original failure evidence and inspect the matching service logs rather than claim a persistent outage or an established cause. Synthetic calculator check: bill ₹1,000, 10% tip, four people produced ₹275 each and ₹1,100 total.
5. **Review private-card boundaries separately.** `/ebusiness-card` has little public explanatory content and requires sign-in for creation. Its public landing should clearly explain the feature and examples. Private profiles/account routes need their own indexing, access and ad-placement rules. Do not mark every short category page noindex or add filler to meet an invented word-count threshold.

The tool pages already expose explanatory text, examples and limitations in server-rendered HTML. Calling the entire property unreadable without JavaScript would be incorrect. Interactive controls appear after hydration on the sampled tools.

## AI Insights

Scope: homepage and a **30-page sample** selected from homepage links first and sitemap URLs second, including 26 articles and About/Contact/Privacy plus the `/blog` redirect. There were 92 discovered internal URLs, so this is not an exhaustive archive audit. Article pages in the sample returned 200; `/blog` returned a 307 navigation redirect and should not be reported as an article error merely because it redirects.

- The sampled guides do contain external citations, explicit hypothetical examples and concrete worksheets/checklists. Do not treat every repeated navigation label or shared author section as scaled-content evidence.
- `What you’ll learn` and shared author/related-reading labels appear across all 26 sampled articles. Thirteen include `Turn the guide into a tested workflow`. Five have the exact `Your first 15 minutes` heading and four the same `Cost, time, privacy, and reliability tradeoffs` heading. Some repetition comes from the site shell; assess the main instructional content separately. Recommend varying article formats only where the actual task benefits: a measured test report, a troubleshooting log, a worked comparison or a maintained implementation walkthrough.
- The current article URLs often contain `auto3h` and timestamp suffixes. That naming alone is not a policy violation or proof of low value. Do not change existing URLs solely to hide the suffix; assess whether a distinct, evidenced reader task justified each publication.
- The Decisions API article's product announcement and 22 October 2026 date are supported by OpenAI's primary announcement: https://openai.com/index/decisions-api/ . This audit did **not** establish that the feature was invented. It did not validate every request field, code example or provider claim across the archive.
- A selected Google Workspace citation encountered a Google verification/challenge redirect through the research tool. That prevents this pass from certifying that particular claim; it does not prove the article or cited page is false. Keep failed-source status separate from disproved claims.
- The agentic-shopping guide explicitly distinguishes vendor framing, approved merchant access and discovery from autonomous purchase. It is more qualified than the root's newer UPI article, which the prepared root batch corrects. Topic overlap does not establish a duplicate; no cross-domain canonical was changed based on title similarity.

Recommended next work in the AI Insights project: review all remaining articles against their cited primary sources, test copyable examples in controlled environments, and promote a small set of genuine first-hand test reports with dates, conditions and reproducible inputs. Avoid commissioning another mass rewrite based only on repeated H2 labels. New evidence must come from an actual test; no invented screenshots, executions, savings or credentials.
