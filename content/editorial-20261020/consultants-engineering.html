Build a decision tree for engagement fit

A consultant's enquiry process should test whether the problem is within scope before asking for a long narrative. A management consultant might ask which process needs attention, whether the sponsor can authorise the work and the approximate team involved. A specialist implementation adviser may need to know the existing system and the desired change. These are illustrative qualification choices, not a recommendation to gather confidential strategy documents on a public form.

Use conditional fields sparingly and validate them again on the server. If the visitor chooses a category outside the firm's remit, provide a clear explanation rather than force them through irrelevant questions. Keep an unknown option where uncertainty is reasonable. The point is to make the first discussion more useful, not to create a scoring system that silently rejects every visitor whose brief does not match a rigid template.

Show an engagement method without disclosing client work

A useful example can describe inputs, decisions, deliverables and the review process without revealing the client's identity. Label a worked hypothetical as hypothetical. Do not invent a named client, revenue improvement or a testimonial to make the page sound authoritative. Where results were measured and publication is permitted, state the observation period, baseline and important limitations so the reader can interpret the claim.

A deliverable preview can be a redacted diagram or a sample checklist prepared specifically for public use. Check downloads for hidden comments, revision history and document metadata before uploading them. A screenshot may also reveal names or private tabs. Treat publication as a review step with an owner, rather than assume that removing the obvious company logo makes every artifact safe to share.

Keep scheduling, consent and lead triage in separate states

A contact request does not automatically authorise newsletter marketing, and a calendar click does not establish an accepted engagement. Record the submitted request, any explicit communication preferences and the scheduled conversation as distinct events. The confirmation should explain what has happened and who will respond. A prospect who returns after a failed connection should not be encouraged to submit the same business brief repeatedly.

For regulated professional services, have the responsible professional review website claims, solicitation and confidentiality obligations before publication. CA practices need a specific review of current ICAI guidance; generic management consultants should not copy a CA firm's restrictions or imply an accounting qualification they do not hold. The technology can support a reviewed process, but it cannot determine professional eligibility from a service label.

At handover, test routing with a realistic in-scope request, an unsuitable request and a staff absence. Someone should own each accepted enquiry and its response deadline. These small operational checks turn an attractive biography into a dependable entry point for the consulting practice.
